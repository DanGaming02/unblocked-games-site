How to Play

TOUCH

Move — left-side joystick
Look — drag on the right side
Place / eat / use — tap anywhere
Break block — press & hold
Fight zombies — tap or hold on them
Jump — round button · Pause — top-right
Inventory — grid button next to the hotbar

SMELTING & THE FURNACE

Ores drop raw items. Craft a Furnace (8 cobblestone,
hollow square), place it and tap it to open it.
Top slot: what to smelt · bottom slot: fuel
Right slot: take the result.
Smelt raw iron & raw gold into ingots, sand into glass,
cobble into stone, logs into charcoal.
Fuels: coal/charcoal = 8 items, planks & logs = 1.5, sticks = 0.5.
A burning furnace glows — and keeps smelting while you're away!

PICKAXE TIERS

Iron ore needs a stone pickaxe to drop raw iron.
Gold & diamond need an iron pickaxe.
Tools: wood → stone → iron → diamond,
plus gold (super fast, but fragile!).
Craft a golden apple (apple + 4 gold ingots) — full heal!

INVENTORY

Tap an item to pick it up, tap a slot to place,
merge or swap stacks.
Hold & slide a stack across slots to split it —
one item drops into every box you sweep over!
The 2×2 crafting grid is in your pocket inventory;
craft a Crafting Table, place it and tap it
for the big 3×3 grid where tools are made.

LIGHT & FOOD

Light floods through the world — torches throw warm glow,
leaves and glass leak soft light, caves are pitch black.
Hold a torch and it lights your way like a lantern!
Leaves sometimes drop apples — hold one and
tap / right-click to eat it and restore hearts.

WEATHER & WORLD

It sometimes rains — watch for lightning!
Zombies don't burn in the rain, so be careful.
Sand falls when nothing holds it up.
Everything is saved automatically in this browser.
"New World" instantly generates a fresh seed — no reload.

SURVIVAL / CREATIVE

Survival: start with nothing — punch trees, mine,
smelt, build shelter. Zombies hunt at night. No flying!
Creative: every block & item, unlimited, instant breaking —
double-tap jump / Space to fly.
Switch modes anytime in the pause menu.

KEYBOARD & MOUSE

Move — WASD   Jump — Space
Break / attack — hold left mouse   Place / eat / use — right mouse
Pick block — middle mouse   Sprint — Ctrl
Select — 1…9 or wheel   Inventory — E
