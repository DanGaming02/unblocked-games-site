AIM FOR RED. PLAY FOR KEEPS.FREE PEERJS MULTIPLAYER · NO PAID SERVER

A bottle. A battle.

  1. Create or join a lobby. The host picks a 2–8 player limit. Everyone starts with 100 health, and a random player goes first.
  2. Click Flip or press Space. Stop the moving marker near the red center for up to a 95% chance of landing. Green edges give only 12%. Even a perfect hit can miss.
  3. Land to build your stack. Each successful flip adds 10 damage. You keep your turn and must flip again.
  4. Miss to unleash it. The next living player in the roster takes the entire stack. It resets to zero, and the next surviving player takes their turn.
  5. Be the last one standing. Zero health means elimination. The host can start a rematch with a fresh random starter.
Keep the host’s tab open. A player who disconnects forfeits; leaving on your turn passes your stack to the next player. Some restricted networks can block direct peer connections.
